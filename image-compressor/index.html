Complete Guide to Image Compressor

High-resolution photos from modern smartphones and digital cameras can easily weigh 5 MB to 15 MB each. Heavy images slow down website loading speeds, fail to upload on job portals and government forms, and quickly consume mobile email attachments. TECHTIS Image Compressor solves this challenge directly inside your browser.

Unlike cloud image compressors like TinyPNG or iLoveIMG that require sending your personal family photos or confidential scans to remote commercial servers, TECHTIS processes image compression entirely in your computer or phone's local RAM. Using hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas bicubic resampling, TECHTIS strips redundant EXIF metadata and optimizes image quantization with zero server latency.

Take full control over your compression ratio with our interactive quality slider, compare before-and-after image byte savings in real time, and download optimized files ready for web publishing, email sending, and online form submissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Image Compressor and privacy

How does in-browser image compression work?
TECHTIS loads your image into local HTML5 Canvas memory and re-encodes it at your selected quality level using client-side browser APIs. Your photo is never sent to any server.
Will compressing my image reduce its physical pixel dimensions?
No, the compression tool reduces file size in kilobytes by optimizing color quantization and removing metadata, without altering pixel dimensions.
What is the recommended quality setting for web images?
A quality level between 75% and 85% provides significant file size reduction (often 60-80% savings) with no discernible loss in visual quality.
Can I compress WebP and PNG images as well as JPG?
Yes! TECHTIS supports JPG, PNG, and modern WebP image formats.
