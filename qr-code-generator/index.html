Complete Guide to QR Code Generator

QR codes are an essential digital bridge between physical marketing materials and web experiences. Whether you are generating a link for business cards, product packaging, restaurant menus, or connecting guests to your Wi-Fi network, TECHTIS QR Code Generator gives you complete styling freedom without advertisements, scan limits, or expired links.

Our client-side matrix algorithm generates standard ISO/IEC 18004 QR codes directly inside your browser memory. You can select custom foreground and background colors, adjust the pixel size, and export both high-resolution PNG raster files and infinitely scalable SVG vector files suitable for high-DPI commercial printing.

Because all code synthesis runs in your device's browser, your confidential Wi-Fi passwords, contact information, and internal URLs are never logged or stored on any external server.
