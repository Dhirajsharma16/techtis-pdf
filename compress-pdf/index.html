Complete Guide to Compress PDF

Email services, job application portals, and government websites often enforce strict file size limits (such as 2MB or 5MB) on uploaded PDF documents. TECHTIS Compress PDF provides a client-side solution that optimizes document structures, re-encodes embedded image streams, and cleans unused metadata tags.

Unlike cloud compressors that queue your document on remote servers, TECHTIS optimizes your PDF directly in your browser's WebAssembly sandbox. You achieve massive size reductions in milliseconds, with no network bottlenecks or upload wait times.

Our compression algorithms balance file size reduction with visual fidelity, ensuring that typography remains sharp and vector diagrams stay crisp for printing and on-screen reading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Compress PDF and privacy

Will compressing my PDF make the text blurry or unreadable?
No. Our compression focuses on stream optimization, metadata stripping, and efficient image compression, ensuring text and vector lines stay crisp.
How much can TECHTIS reduce my PDF file size?
Depending on the content (e.g. scanned images vs vector text), reductions range from 30% to 85% of the original file size.
Is it safe to compress confidential bank statements or contracts?
Yes, 100%. All processing happens strictly in your browser RAM. No bytes are sent across the internet to any external server.
Can I compress password-protected PDF files?
You will need to unlock the PDF first using our Unlock PDF tool before compressing, so the compression engine can parse document streams.
