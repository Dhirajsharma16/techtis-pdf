Complete Guide to Fill Form (OCR Assistant)

Filling out government applications, university admissions, tax portals, job applications, and visa forms is often tedious and repetitive. Applicants constantly switch between document viewers, image previews, and web forms to manually copy-paste names, addresses, identification numbers, and contact details.

TECHTIS Fill Form Assistant provides a unified, private workflow designed specifically for rapid form filling. Simply upload any PDF document, scanned identity card, salary slip, or certificate. Our dual-engine client-side parser automatically extracts key fields including Full Name, Email, Mobile Phone, Date of Birth, Gender, Document Numbers, Father's Name, PIN Code, and Full Address.

With our innovative "Dock to Right" mode, TECHTIS shrinks into an accessible side assistant fixed to the right side of your screen. This gives you clear space to open and interact with any target application portal (such as Passport Seva, Income Tax e-Filing, EPFO, or Google Forms) while keeping your document's details readily available for instantaneous one-click copying.

Most importantly, TECHTIS runs optical character recognition and document analysis 100% locally in your web browser using WebAssembly. Your confidential identity credentials, financial documents, and personal details never leave your computer or touch any cloud server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fill Form (OCR Assistant) and privacy

What types of documents can I upload to the Fill Form tool?
You can upload both PDF documents (single or multi-page) and images (JPG, PNG, WebP) including Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, passports, driving licences, utility bills, marksheets, and resumes.
How does "Fill Form Mode (Dock to Right)" work?
Clicking the "Fill Form Mode (Dock to Right)" button transforms the tool into a compact 380px floating panel docked on the right side of your screen. This leaves the left side of your browser viewport open for navigating online forms and application portals.
Are my private documents sent to an external AI or OCR server?
No! All processing runs 100% locally on your device using client-side WebAssembly and PDF.js. Zero bytes of your documents or extracted information are uploaded to our servers or third-party cloud APIs.
Can I edit or add information before copying?
Yes. Every extracted field is fully editable, and you can also copy the entire document text or all extracted fields simultaneously with a single click.
