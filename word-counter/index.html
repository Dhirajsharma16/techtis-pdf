Complete Guide to Word & Character Counter

Whether you are writing an academic essay with strict word limits, crafting an SEO blog post, preparing a keynote speech, or fitting a social media post within platform character limits, accurate text analytics are vital. TECHTIS Word & Character Counter provides real-time statistics as you type.

Our analysis engine tracks total words, characters with spaces, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, estimated reading duration, and speaking time. A built-in keyword frequency analyzer identifies your most commonly used words, helping you avoid repetitive phrasing and optimize content for search engines.

Because all text processing executes in your browser's local memory, confidential manuscripts, legal briefs, and personal emails remain strictly private.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Word & Character Counter and privacy

How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is calculated based on the standard average reading speed of 225 words per minute (wpm).
Does this tool ignore common words like "the" in keyword density?
Yes! Our frequency engine filters out English stop words (and, the, is, in, to, of, for, with) to highlight your meaningful content keywords.
Is there any limit to how much text I can paste?
Because calculations run on your device CPU, you can paste entire books or 100-page dissertations without slowing down.
