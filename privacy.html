Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately for all visitors worldwide

100% In-Browser RAM

PDFs, images, and identity cards are processed strictly inside your device memory via WebAssembly and Canvas.

Zero Server Uploads

We maintain no file servers or databases. Files are never transmitted over the Internet to TECHTIS.

Instant Memory Purge

Closing your browser tab or clicking reset immediately clears all document buffers from your computer's RAM.

1. Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

At TECHTIS (techtis.in), privacy is not an afterthought or an optional toggle—it is the foundational engineering principle of our entire platform.

Most online file conversion services require you to transmit personal documents over the network to remote backend servers. TECHTIS eliminates this privacy vulnerability entirely by conducting 100% of document parsing, image rendering, merging, form filling, compression, and format transcoding directly inside your device's local memory.

2. Document & File Data Handling

  • No Server Transmission: When you drag, drop, or select a file in any of our 60 tools, the file is read exclusively into the JavaScript heap memory of your local web browser.
  • No File Logging or Caching: TECHTIS operates zero file-storage buckets, zero object storage (such as AWS S3 or Cloudflare R2), and zero intermediate processing queues.
  • Immediate Destruction: When you complete an operation and download your file, or when you refresh/close the webpage, your browser's garbage collector automatically overwrites and reclaims the memory.

3. Special Privacy Provisions for Sensitive Tools

Certain tools in our suite handle exceptionally sensitive personal materials:

  • Aadhaar & PAN Card OCR: Text extraction from Indian identification cards runs strictly on-device using WebAssembly compiled OCR. No card numbers, names, or photos are ever sent to remote AI services unless the user explicitly toggles a cloud AI option with their own personal API key.
  • Passport Photo Maker: Camera captures and uploaded portrait photographs are cropped, scaled, and tiled onto printable 4x6 / A4 sheets exclusively within client-side HTML5 Canvas.
  • PDF Form Filler: Flattened vectors, text stamps, and digital signatures are burned directly into the PDF byte stream using pdf-lib in client RAM.

4. Browser LocalStorage & Cookies

TECHTIS utilizes standard browser localStorage strictly for user convenience:

  • Activity Counter: A local counter of completed operations is saved on your device so you can view your personal productivity history. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time in the Activity Log modal.
  • Custom API Keys: If you optionally supply personal API keys (such as Google Gemini, Groq, or OCR.space), they are saved exclusively in your browser's local storage and used solely for direct client-to-API requests.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Partners

To keep all 60 document and image utilities 100% free with unlimited usage worldwide, TECHTIS may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense and reputable advertising networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to TECHTIS and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.
  • Crucially: Advertisers and advertising scripts have absolutely NO access to your uploaded documents, images, or extracted text, which remain isolated inside sandboxed client memory.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Data Rights

Because TECHTIS does not collect, store, or transmit your personal files or personal identifiers, there is no database of user records to export, modify, or erase under GDPR or CCPA. You retain 100% data sovereignty at all times.
