1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

TECHTIS (techtis.in) was engineered from the ground up on a radical privacy paradigm: the most secure server is no server at all. Traditional document conversion websites force users to upload sensitive tax returns, confidential legal agreements, and personal photos to remote cloud servers. TECHTIS eliminates this risk completely by executing 100% of PDF manipulation, image processing, and character recognition directly in the visitor's local web browser engine.

2. Document & File Processing

When you select a document or image on TECHTIS:

  • Your file is read exclusively into local client memory via JavaScript FileReader, HTMLCanvasElement, or WebAssembly buffers.
  • Operations (such as merging, splitting, compressing, cropping, passport photo tiling, and ID card OCR) execute entirely on your computer's CPU and GPU.
  • No document data, pixel data, or metadata is ever saved, logged, or uploaded to TECHTIS servers or any third-party storage buckets.
  • When you close your browser tab or click "Clear All Data", all working buffers are instantly released by your browser's garbage collector.

3. Identity Documents & Private OCR

Our Private Document OCR tool (/id-card-ocr/) is specifically built for sensitive government identity cards (including Indian Aadhaar and PAN cards):

  • Optical character recognition executes strictly client-side via in-browser Tesseract.js WebAssembly.
  • Sensitive 12-digit Aadhaar numbers are masked by default (XXXX XXXX 1234) to protect against accidental visual shoulder-surfing.
  • A prominent "Clear All Data" control allows users to instantly wipe recognized data and canvas buffers from RAM.

4. Browser LocalStorage & Cookies

TECHTIS uses standard browser localStorage strictly for your convenience:

  • Activity Counter: A local counter of completed operations is saved on your device so you can view your personal productivity history. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time in the Activity Log modal.
  • Custom API Keys: If you optionally supply personal API keys (such as Google Gemini, Groq, or OCR.space), they are saved exclusively in your browser's local storage and used solely for direct client-to-API requests.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Partners

To keep all 40 document and image utilities 100% free with unlimited usage worldwide, TECHTIS may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense and reputable advertising networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to TECHTIS and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.
  • Crucially: Advertisers and advertising scripts have absolutely NO access to your uploaded documents, images, or extracted text, which remain isolated inside sandboxed client memory.
