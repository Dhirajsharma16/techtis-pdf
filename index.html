Eliminate slow upload bars and download queues. Tasks execute at raw CPU hardware speeds directly in system RAM, making document operations virtually instantaneous.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our serverless, client-side PDF platform.

Is TECHTIS completely free to use worldwide?

Yes, TECHTIS is 100% free with no hidden subscriptions, credit card requirements, or artificial limits. Users across all countries can merge, split, compress, fill forms, and convert unlimited files without paying any fees.

Are my files safe and private when using TECHTIS?

Absolutely. Unlike traditional online PDF websites that upload your files to remote cloud storage, TECHTIS executes every operation directly inside your web browser's local RAM memory. Your private documents never leave your device and never touch a backend server.

How do I fill flat forms or Aadhaar forms that lack interactive digital fields?

TECHTIS Form Filler includes a visual click-to-type engine. Simply click anywhere on any PDF - including flat scanned government forms like Aadhaar or PAN card applications - to type text, place checkmarks (X), and stamp dates, permanently burning them into the PDF at exact coordinates.

Can I use TECHTIS on smartphones, tablets, and computers?

Yes. TECHTIS is fully responsive and functions seamlessly across all modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux without needing to install apps or browser extensions.

Is there any file size limit or daily document limit?

Because all processing takes place locally on your computer or phone hardware, there are no artificial caps or daily document restrictions. You can process as many pages and files as your device's memory supports.
