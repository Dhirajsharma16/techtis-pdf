Complete Guide to Merge PDF

Combining multiple PDF documents into a single consolidated file is essential for preparing business proposals, compiling invoices, submitting multi-page job applications, and organizing academic research. While traditional cloud converters require uploading your confidential files to remote servers, TECHTIS processes your entire PDF merger locally in your device's memory.

Our client-side WebAssembly engine reads each document's byte stream, merges cross-reference tables, consolidates embedded fonts, and rebuilds the catalog hierarchy directly in RAM. Whether you are merging two contracts or assembling fifty scanned receipts, your confidential data never leaves your computer or phone.

With intuitive drag-and-drop file reordering, you can arrange files in the exact sequence you need before generating the final output. TECHTIS guarantees zero server latency, zero bandwidth consumption, and total confidentiality for all legal, medical, and financial documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Merge PDF and privacy

Is there a limit to how many PDF files I can merge at once?
Because merging runs locally in your device memory rather than on a congested cloud server, there is no artificial limit on the number of files or total page count. You can easily merge dozens of PDFs at once.
Are my confidential documents uploaded to any server?
No. TECHTIS operates on a strict zero-upload architecture. All file parsing, byte reordering, and PDF assembly happen inside your browser using client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly.
Can I reorder the PDF files before merging?
Yes. Simply drag and drop the file cards in the preview workspace to place them in your desired order before clicking the Merge button.
Does TECHTIS work on mobile phones?
Yes! TECHTIS works smoothly on iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and all modern mobile web browsers without installing any apps.
