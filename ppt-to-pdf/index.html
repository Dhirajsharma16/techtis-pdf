Complete Guide to PowerPoint to PDF

Distributing slide decks in native PowerPoint (.pptx) format often results in font substitution glitches, broken layout alignments, or oversized email attachments. Furthermore, attendees on mobile devices or computers without Microsoft Office may struggle to open presentation files. Converting your deck to PDF creates a universal, lightweight handout.

TECHTIS PowerPoint to PDF unzips the presentation container and parses slide XML elements directly in browser memory. It compiles each slide's headings, bulleted lists, and notes into structured PDF pages ready for printing or emailing to clients and conference attendees.
