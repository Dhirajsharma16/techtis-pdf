Complete Guide to Sign PDF

In modern business, printing out contracts, signing them with a pen, scanning them back to a computer, and emailing them is an outdated, time-consuming chore. TECHTIS Sign PDF lets you electronically sign agreements, lease forms, NDAs, and onboarding documents in seconds right from your browser.

Our responsive HTML5 canvas pad captures natural, high-resolution handwritten strokes whether you use a mouse, touchpad, or smartphone touchscreen. Alternatively, you can type your name to generate an elegant calligraphic script signature or upload a transparent image of your existing stamp.

Once placed, your signature is embedded permanently into the PDF's vector stream. Because all processing executes in local browser RAM, your sensitive legal agreements, personal signatures, and identification documents remain 100% private and protected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sign PDF and privacy

Is an electronic signature generated by TECHTIS legally binding?
In most jurisdictions worldwide (including the US ESIGN Act, EU eIDAS regulation, and India IT Act), electronic signatures are legally valid for most general business agreements, contracts, and forms.
Can I sign using my finger on a smartphone or tablet?
Yes! Our signature pad is fully optimized for touchscreens on iOS and Android devices.
Is my handwritten signature stored on your servers?
Never. Your signature is processed exclusively in your device's local memory and is never saved, transmitted, or logged.
Can I add initials and today's date alongside my signature?
Yes, you can create multiple signature stamps, initials, and date tags and position each onto the document.
