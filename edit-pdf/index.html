Complete Guide to Edit PDF

Making quick edits, highlighting crucial clauses, adding review notes, or typing corrections on existing PDF files usually requires clunky desktop software or paid monthly subscriptions. TECHTIS Edit PDF delivers a lightweight, browser-based annotation editor that lets you markup any PDF with ease.

Our editor provides an intuitive toolbar equipped with text insertion, rectangular highlights, arrows, and custom shapes. You can easily comment on student papers, mark up architectural drafts, annotate contract terms, or add supplementary notes to financial spreadsheets.

All edits are composited onto the original PDF vector canvas in browser memory, maintaining original font crispness and image clarity while guaranteeing complete privacy for your sensitive documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Edit PDF and privacy

How to add text, highlights, and shapes to a PDF online for free?
Open your PDF in TECHTIS Edit PDF. Use the toolbar to click anywhere to type text, choose the highlighter to mark sentences, or draw shapes (arrows, rectangles, circles). When finished, click 'Save PDF' to download your annotated document.
How can I blackout or redact private details in a PDF?
Select the rectangle shape tool, choose a solid black fill color, and draw the box over sensitive information like Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, or account balances. Then use Flatten PDF to permanently merge the redaction.
Can I type new text onto an existing PDF?
Yes. Click the Text tool, click on any empty or existing area of the document, and type your desired text with customizable font size, color, and alignment.
Do I need to install Adobe Acrobat or pay a monthly fee?
No! TECHTIS is 100% free in your browser with zero downloads, no credit card, and no watermarks.
Are my confidential documents uploaded to any remote server?
No. All annotation and editing operations execute locally in your browser memory via WebAssembly. Your files remain strictly private.
Can I draw with a freehand pen or stylus?
Yes. Our drawing tool allows freehand markup, signatures, and sketches using mouse, finger touch, or stylus.
Does this tool work on mobile phones and iPads?
Yes! Full touch support allows you to highlight and annotate documents smoothly on iOS and Android devices.
Will editing degrade the resolution of scanned pages?
No. Background scans and vector graphics remain untouched; annotations are added as high-resolution native layers.
