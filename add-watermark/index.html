Complete Guide to Add Watermark

Protecting intellectual property, preventing unauthorized redistribution, and clearly identifying document status (such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, or FOR REVIEW ONLY) requires visible watermarking. TECHTIS Add Watermark lets you stamp clear, semi-transparent text watermarks across every page of your PDF in seconds.

Our intuitive controls give you total control over styling: adjust font size, change color, set custom rotation angles (such as standard 45-degree diagonal watermarks), and dial in the exact transparency level so the watermark remains legible without obscuring underlying document text.

Because all watermarking is calculated and rendered directly in your browser's WebAssembly sandbox, your sensitive business plans, creative manuscripts, and confidential contracts never travel across the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Add Watermark and privacy

Will the watermark obscure the underlying text of my document?
No, you can adjust the opacity slider (typically 15% to 30%) so the watermark is clearly visible while keeping the underlying text completely legible.
Can I apply watermarks to specific pages only?
By default, the watermark is applied across all pages, but you can specify a page range subset if needed.
Can the watermark be easily removed by recipients?
TECHTIS stamps the watermark directly into the PDF content stream, making it permanent when combined with our Flatten PDF tool.
Is this tool free and private?
Yes, 100% free with no account required, processing exclusively inside your local browser memory.
