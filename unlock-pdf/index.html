Complete Guide to Unlock PDF

Many utility bills, bank statements, tax documents, and corporate records are delivered with password protection. While helpful during transmission, typing a complex password every time you need to view, print, or attach the document to an expense report becomes a frustrating burden. TECHTIS Unlock PDF lets you remove passwords and restrictions permanently.

By providing the document password once, our client-side decryption engine removes the cryptographic wrapper and generates an unrestricted PDF copy that opens instantly in any viewer without prompts. Furthermore, it strips frustrating owner restrictions that block printing, annotating, or copying text.

Because all decryption executes inside your browser sandbox, your sensitive banking passwords and confidential financial records are never transmitted to third-party servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Unlock PDF and privacy

Do I need to know the password to unlock the document?
Yes. For encrypted PDFs with open passwords, you must enter the correct password once so the browser can decrypt and save an unlocked copy.
Does this tool remove printing and copying restrictions?
Yes. It strips all owner-level permission restrictions, allowing you to freely copy text, annotate, and print your document.
Are my financial or bank passwords sent to your server?
No! All decryption operations take place locally in your device's browser memory. Zero data is sent across the network.
Can I edit the PDF after unlocking it?
Yes. The unlocked PDF is fully editable with our Edit PDF, Form Filler, and Split PDF tools.
