Complete Guide to OCR PDF (Scan to Text)

Scanned documents and photos of printed papers are essentially static pictures of text—you cannot search for keywords, highlight sentences, or copy text into word processors. Retyping pages manually wastes hours of productive time. TECHTIS OCR PDF runs an Optical Character Recognition engine directly inside your web browser using WebAssembly.

Our engine inspects pixel patterns, isolates lines of print, and reconstructs character streams with high precision. It handles multi-column layouts, tabular numbers, and diverse typography styles. Once recognized, you can copy text with one click or export it for editing.

Unlike cloud OCR services that store your scans on remote databases, TECHTIS processes your scans locally on your computer's CPU, keeping medical records, personal identity scans, and financial receipts completely private.
