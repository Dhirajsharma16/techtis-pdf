Complete Guide to Repair PDF

Incomplete downloads, USB drive corruption, failed email attachments, or software crashes can corrupt PDF files, leaving them unreadable by standard software like Adobe Acrobat or browser viewers. When an essential document cannot be opened, having an automated repair utility is invaluable.

TECHTIS Repair PDF inspects the raw binary streams of the damaged document, bypassing broken cross-reference (XREF) tables and missing trailer tags. It identifies surviving object streams, reconstructs the document catalog, and generates a valid, standard-compliant PDF file that opens smoothly in all viewers.

Because the repair process takes place entirely in browser RAM, corrupted business contracts, medical scans, and personal records are recovered with absolute privacy and zero cloud exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Repair PDF and privacy

Can TECHTIS fix all corrupted PDF files?
TECHTIS can successfully repair files with damaged cross-reference tables, missing EOF markers, corrupted headers, and stream offsets. Severely overwritten or zero-byte files cannot be recovered.
Why do PDF files get corrupted?
Common causes include interrupted downloads, unexpected power cuts while saving, failing storage drives, or improper email attachment encoding.
Are my recovered files sent to any remote server?
No. Binary stream parsing and reconstruction happen exclusively in your browser's local memory.
Does repairing a PDF cost anything?
No, TECHTIS PDF Repair is 100% free with unlimited uses.
