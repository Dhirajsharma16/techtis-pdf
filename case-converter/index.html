Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Case Converter and privacy

What is Title Case?
Title Case capitalizes the first letter of major words while keeping minor prepositions and conjunctions (a, an, the, in, of, on) lowercase.
What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?
camelCase starts with a lowercase letter (e.g. myVariableName), whereas PascalCase capitalizes the first letter of every word (e.g. MyVariableName).
Can I convert code variable lists?
Yes! Simply paste your list of words or variables and click snake_case or camelCase to format them instantly.
