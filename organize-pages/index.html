Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Organize Pages and privacy

How do I rearrange pages using this tool?
Simply click and hold any page thumbnail, drag it to the desired position in the grid, and release. The page order updates in real time.
Can I rotate or delete pages while organizing?
Yes! Each page thumbnail tile features quick action icons to rotate 90 degrees or delete the page directly.
Is there a limit on how many pages can be organized?
You can organize documents with hundreds of pages smoothly, limited only by your computer's available RAM.
Does reorganizing alter the text formatting or vector lines?
Not in the slightest. The content streams of the pages remain bit-for-bit identical; only their order in the document catalog is modified.
