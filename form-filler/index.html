Complete Guide to Form Filler

Most government forms, school registration sheets, and bank loan applications are distributed as static, flat scanned PDFs with no interactive fillable fields. Users are often forced to print them out, handwrite in tiny boxes, and scan them back in. TECHTIS Form Filler solves this problem with an intelligent click-to-type visual engine that lets you fill any PDF form directly on screen.

Simply click anywhere on a blank line, box, or margin to start typing. Adjust font size to fit neatly within constrained boxes, toggle checkmarks (✓ or X) for checkboxes, and insert today's date with a single click. For official PDFs that contain native AcroForm digital fields, TECHTIS reads and highlights them automatically.

Because government applications and financial declarations contain your most sensitive personally identifiable information (PII), TECHTIS processes everything 100% client-side in browser RAM with zero server communication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Form Filler and privacy

How do I fill a scanned PDF that has no interactive form fields?
With TECHTIS, simply click directly on any blank line or box on the document canvas, and an interactive text box appears where you can type immediately.
Can I mark checkboxes and radio buttons?
Yes! Select the Checkmark or Cross tool from the toolbar and click inside any square box or circular radio button to stamp it.
Is it safe to enter sensitive data like passport or tax identification numbers?
Yes, 100%. TECHTIS operates entirely in your local browser sandbox; no form data or documents are ever uploaded to remote servers.
Can I add my signature to the form after filling it?
Yes! You can switch seamlessly to our Sign tool to add your signature and date before exporting the finished form.
