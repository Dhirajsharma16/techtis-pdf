Complete Guide to Split PDF

Splitting large PDF documents into focused individual sections is a daily necessity for legal discovery, tax preparation, academic research, and client reporting. TECHTIS Split PDF gives you total control over how pages are extracted, allowing you to isolate exact page ranges (like pages 3–7 and 12) or split a 100-page catalog into individual single-page documents.

Our built-in thumbnail rendering engine gives you an immediate visual preview of every page in your document. You can visually inspect pages before deciding which sections to extract. When splitting multiple pages, TECHTIS uses client-side JSZip to bundle the resulting documents into a convenient ZIP archive in seconds.

Because all operations run locally in your browser, you never have to wait for large multi-megabyte files to upload over slow connections. Experience desktop-class splitting performance directly in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Split PDF and privacy

How do I specify custom page ranges when splitting?
You can type custom page ranges in standard notation (e.g., "1-4, 8, 11-15") or click directly on the interactive thumbnail grid to select pages.
Can I split every page into a separate PDF file?
Yes. Choose the "Extract All Pages" mode, and TECHTIS will generate a standalone PDF for each page and package them into a single ZIP file.
Will the visual quality or text clarity degrade after splitting?
Not at all. The internal vector graphics, embedded TrueType fonts, and resolution of images remain completely unchanged.
Is there a file size limit for splitting PDFs?
There are no artificial server limits. Your document is processed by your computer's available RAM and CPU.
