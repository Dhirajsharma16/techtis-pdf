Complete Guide to Document Compare

Reviewing legal contracts, vendor agreements, and policy documents after multiple rounds of revisions is stressful. Counterparties often make subtle modifications—altering liability clauses, shifting payment deadlines, or deleting key conditions—without tracking changes. TECHTIS Document Compare extracts text streams from two PDF versions and performs a side-by-side visual diff.

Our diffing algorithm compares words and lines sequentially, highlighting inserted text in green and removed passages in red strikethrough. You can quickly pinpoint every single change between drafts without having to read dozens of pages line-by-line.

Because legal agreements and merger proposals contain highly sensitive trade secrets, TECHTIS executes the entire text comparison locally in your browser RAM. Your confidential drafts are never uploaded to any cloud server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Document Compare and privacy

How does Document Compare highlight changes?
It displays both documents side-by-side, using standard revision colors: green highlights for added text and red strikethrough for deleted text.
Can it detect changed numbers and dates?
Yes! The diff algorithm operates at the word and character level, catching tiny shifts in dollar figures, dates, or liability percentages.
Does this work on scanned documents?
Document Compare requires selectable text streams. For image-only scans, run our OCR PDF tool first to extract searchable text.
Are my confidential legal contracts uploaded to your servers?
Never. Both documents are processed strictly in your local browser memory sandbox.
